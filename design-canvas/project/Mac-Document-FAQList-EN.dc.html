Other Issues

-Restore purchase/Activate account:

1) From Settings on the top left

2) Click Restore Purchases

3) Sign in to iTunes Store with the same Apple ID that you used to purchase the subscription

4) Tap OK to confirm

-Turn off auto-renewal:

1) From App Store, Tap "Featured" at the bottom of the screen

2) Scroll to the bottom of the page

3) Sign in with the same Apple ID associated with the purchase

4) Click "View Apple ID"

5) Enter your password and click OK from Account Settings, click "Manage App Subscriptions"

5) Cancel Securefield VPN subscription
